Plan a Camelback Mountain Family Adventure

Camelback is one of Phoenix's signature hikes, but the summit routes are not casual family walks. Use this page to decide whether Cholla, Echo Canyon, a partial hike, or a completely different Phoenix outing fits your people today.

Should your family hike Camelback?

Both Cholla Trail and Echo Canyon are officially rated Extremely Difficult. That does not automatically mean “never with kids,” but it does mean age alone is a poor test. Experience, heat tolerance, scrambling ability, footwear, water, timing and willingness to turn around matter more.

Maybe, if all of this is true

  • The group has real hiking experience.
  • Everyone is comfortable on steep, rocky terrain.
  • You can start in safe weather and finish before dangerous heat.
  • You have ample water, proper shoes and a hard turnaround rule.
  • No one feels pressure to summit just because other people are doing it.

Choose another plan if any of this is true

  • Young children are new to desert hiking.
  • Someone dislikes heights, exposure or scrambling.
  • You are starting late or the forecast is hot.
  • The group is short on water or proper footwear.
  • Anyone is already tired, sick, dehydrated or reluctant.

Cholla or Echo Canyon?

Neither route is “the easy one.” They are different versions of hard.

Use a four-step family decision

Check the mountain. Verify current closures, trail hours, heat restrictions and weather before anyone gets dressed for the hike.
Check the humans. Use the least-experienced hiker as the baseline. Ask about heights, scrambling, heat, knees, energy and willingness.
Choose the objective. Summit, partial Cholla, scenic Camelback views, or a nearby easier desert outing are all legitimate plans.
Set the turnaround before starting. Pick a time, water threshold and terrain threshold. Turning around is the plan working, not the plan failing.
Useful Cholla landmark: The City of Phoenix describes a saddle/rest area before the upper boulder section. If the group is already working hard there, that is a very reasonable place to call the hike complete.

Before you go

Heat

Start early, check the forecast and skip exposed hiking during dangerous heat. Camelback has very little shade.

Water

Carry enough for the full return plus margin. Do not build the plan around finding water at Cholla.

Parking

Cholla has no dedicated trailhead parking lot. Follow posted legal-parking signs on Invergordon Road and never block neighborhood access.

Frequently asked questions

Is Camelback Mountain good for kids?

It can be appropriate for some experienced older children and teens with prepared adults, but both summit trails are rated Extremely Difficult. Many families will have a better day choosing a partial hike or easier Phoenix trail.

Which is easier, Cholla or Echo Canyon?

Neither is easy. Cholla is longer and becomes very steep and boulder-filled near the top. Echo Canyon is shorter but extremely steep and includes climbing-like sections.

Can we just hike part of Cholla?

Yes, once the trail is open. A partial outing with a planned turnaround can be a much better family objective than committing to the summit.

Are dogs allowed on Camelback Mountain?

No. The City of Phoenix prohibits dogs on Camelback Mountain trails year-round.
